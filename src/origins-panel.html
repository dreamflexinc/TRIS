Equations, assumptions & sources

The Big Bang view numerically integrates a flat FLRW background with radiation, matter and a cosmological constant. Progress is logarithmic in dimensionless cosmic age, compressing very different epochs into one view. Approximate ages use a reference Hubble constant; temperatures follow a cooling proxy and become qualitative at very early times. This begins after the unresolved Planck/inflation regime. Scene distances are log-compressed so early stages remain visible. Clouds, atoms, stars and filaments illustrate epochs; no nuclear network or gravitational N-body evolution is solved.

The Big Bounce view uses a smooth analytic scale factor with a positive minimum. It demonstrates contraction and expansion without claiming a quantum-gravity solution or evidence that our universe bounced.

Primordial soup couples a Gray–Scott concentration field to coarse-grained charged and dipolar bead particles. Zap applies an electrical impulse; stirring drives vortical motion; magnetization rotates dipoles and bends charged-particle motion. Continuous forcing has adjustable angle and polarity. These are abstract, dissipative bead dynamics, not atomistic molecular chemistry or an origin-of-life solver.
