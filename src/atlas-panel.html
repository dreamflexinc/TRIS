Projection, branch cuts & sources

We sample z₁ = r exp(iθ), solve all n roots z₂ⁿ = −1 − z₁ⁿ − cⁿ, then rotate and discard one of the four real coordinates before drawing in 3D. Radius bounds omit most of the affine chart; projective points at infinity are absent. The cubic has no c coordinate.

Surfaces omit long edges across root branch cuts. Gaps and self-intersections in the image are not holes or singularities of the parent manifold. Topology values describe the smooth compact parent, not the mesh or a potentially singular slice. No Ricci-flat metric or string dynamics is computed.
